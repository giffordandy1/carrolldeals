    Sykesville Craft Beer Festival

    Description
    60+ beers for unlimited sampling. Four hours. One Main Street worth raising a glass to!

    🍺 Unlimited craft beer sampling
    🎶 Fantastic live bands
    🌮 Food trucks and local vendors
    🥳 Four hours of pure Main Street fun!


    📅 SATURDAY, NOVEMBER 14 | 12–4 PM


    The 15th Annual Sykesville Craft Beer Festival is bringing unlimited beer sampling, live music by FireKite, food trucks, and artisan vendors to historic downtown!

    Saturday, November 14 | Noon–4 PM

    Guest tickets include $10 in Main Street Bucks to spend at participating downtown businesses.
    Free shuttles, including accessible service for ticket holders.
