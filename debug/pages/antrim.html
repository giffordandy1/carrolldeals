Historic Taneytown, MD Hotel with Award-Winning Dining

Welcome to Antrim 1844 – A Destination Hotel, Restaurant & Event Venue in Taneytown, MD
Luxury Accommodations

Escape to Our Elegantly Restored 19th-Century Estate

Nestled at the foot of the breathtaking Catoctin Mountains in Central Maryland, this beautiful 24-acre estate is renowned for its superb cuisine and upscale Maryland accommodations. Owners Richard and Dorothy Mollett have masterfully restored the property to reflect its original splendor of 1844. Just 20 minutes from Gettysburg and 45 minutes from Frederick, Maryland, Antrim 1844 is the perfect location for romantic getaways, exquisite fine dining, elegant weddings, family reunions, corporate meetings, retreats, and more.

Your stay at our Taneytown, MD hotel will include gracious accommodations, exceptional service, complimentary amenities like a cooked-to-order breakfast and Wi-Fi, and access to our beautiful grounds and resort activities including manicured gardens, sport courts and yard games, and a seasonal swimming pool.

Estate of Luxury

Step Back in Time for an Authentic Experience

 

Being a member of Historic Hotels of America since 2007 and listed on the National Register, Antrim 1844 stands as a beacon of preserved historical elegance, a testament to the estate’s rich history. At the heart of the estate, the Formal English Tea Rose Gardens bloom alongside bronze fountains and historic architecture, earning a spot among the Top 25 Historic Gardens by Historic Hotels of America.

In addition to our gardens and grounds, guests are welcome to enjoy our resort-style amenities, including a seasonal swimming pool, croquet lawn, and sport courts, including pickleball, tennis, volleyball and more. View our area guide and event calendar below to help plan ahead for your stay.
