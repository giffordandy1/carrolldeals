Our Story

Nick & Keith go way back & they've always kept it simple. They set out to open Olde Mother Brewing with the same simplicity in mind; brew good beer, make good friends, and convince a community to join them in their journey to build a beer brand.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is Olde Mother dog friendly?

Since we have a kitchen in our brewery now, we can only allow registered service animals inside. Our patio is still friendly to all!

Can we bring our kids (or anyone under 21)?

Yes! You and all your family and friends are welcome to visit.

Do you have food?

We do! Backslash Burger has opened their kitchen inside the brewery! You can order at the kitchen, via the QR codes throughout the brewery, or by going here. You are also welcome to bring in outside food, just no outside alcoholic beverages.

Do you take reservations/book private events?

At this time, we do not take reservations or book private events.

How do I apply to be a vendor at one of your markets?

We don’t handle the vendor selection for our markets. Check out @keycitythriftexpo on Instagram for Vendor Calls and additional information.

How do I buy an e-gift card?
