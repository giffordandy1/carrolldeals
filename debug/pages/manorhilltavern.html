Our Food

Our Head Chef strives to use seasonal and locally-sourced ingredients whenever possible, including herbs and vegetables from our farm.  Our distinctive menu is highlighted by a custom-built brick oven for Neapolitan-style pizzas and more.

Menu

Our Beer

We proudly serve beers from Manor Hill Brewing, Howard County’s first farm brewery.  Manor Hill beers are hand-made on our farm using original and unique recipes. The result is aromatic, hop forward farmhouse style beers.
