About Ledo Pizza Eldersburg, Maryland

Ledo Pizza Eldersburg is at 1314 Londontown Boulevard. Use this restaurant’s menu to order a pizza for pickup, then add wings, subs, or salad to suit the people you’re feeding.

Square pizza made to order

Our pizza starts with a thin, flaky crust, Ledo sauce, and smoked provolone cheese. Choose your favorite toppings, try one of our Craft Pizzas, or order several combinations to share. Whether you are planning a meal for yourself or feeding the household, you can build your order around the flavors everyone enjoys.

There is more than pizza on the menu at Ledo Pizza Eldersburg. Add wings to share, choose a sub for your meal, or explore our pasta and salad options. Check this location’s menu for current selections and prices before you order.

Order from Ledo Pizza Eldersburg

For a meal at home or food to bring to your next gathering, order online from Ledo Pizza Eldersburg or call 410-552-4000. Choose this restaurant when placing your order so your pickup is prepared at the address below.

Catering for gatherings in Eldersburg

Planning a team party, office lunch, school function, or family celebration? Call our restaurant to discuss a catering order. Pizza, pasta, subs, salads, and wings give you options for different appetites and group sizes. Let us know your guest count and event time so our team can help you plan quantities, review menu choices, and arrange your pickup.

Contact us in advance for larger orders and ask about availability for your preferred date. Our team will confirm the details with you before your catering order is finalized.
