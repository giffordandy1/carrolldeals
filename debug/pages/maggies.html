Completely Renovated in 2021

Maggie’s Restaurant in Westminster is a Carroll County tradition of hospitality and great food for over 44 years. Maggie’s was completely renovated in 2021. Enjoy outdoor dining on our patio or dine in one of our newly redecorated dining rooms. We can host your private event or party in our new private event space.

Happy Hour & Sunday Brunch

Come enjoy Happy Hour Sunday-Thursday from 3-6 pm in bar with live music on Friday 7 -10 pm and don’t forget Carroll Counties best brunch on Saturday and Sunday! An old-fashioned space with a deck, patio & woodsy pub.
