our story

RAK Brewing: Your Sanctuary for Classic Elegance and Relaxation

Discover RAK Brewing, where a classic and sophisticated ambiance meets a relaxed atmosphere. Nestled at the perfect elevation, our brewery offers a unique blend of comfort and style, making everyone feel right at home.

Unlike any other brewery, RAK is an inclusive and friendly community hub where people gather to enjoy great times, fantastic company, and exceptional brews.

Committed to our community, RAK Brewing embodies the spirit of Random Acts of Kindness. We proudly take our name from this dedication to giving back and paying it forward, setting a new standard for what a brewery can achieve.

🍻 THREE NEW BEERS DROPPING TODAY! 🍻

We’re kicking off the weekend with a little hop magic, a little holiday cheer, and THREE fresh releases at RAK! 🔥🎄

🍺 Riwaka | Hazy IPA | 7.4%
All Riwaka, all gas, no brakes! Double dry-hopped and bursting with passion fruit, grapefruit, citrus, and tropical funk. Juicy, bold, and pillowy smooth!

💚 Resting Grinch Face | Smoothie Sour | 6.5%
Holiday stress? We’ve got a beer for that. Juicy pear, zesty lime, and smooth vanilla come together in this thick, creamy sour that’ll make even the Grinch crack a smile.

🥧 Plopstar Pie | Smoothie Sour | 6.5%
Your favorite Plopstar is back with a holiday glow-up! Cranberry, orange, and cozy pie-inspired flavors make this one dessert in a glass.
