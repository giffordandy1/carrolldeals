About Frisco

Since its humble beginnings in 1996, Frisco has been the heartbeat of our local community. What began as Frisco Burrito in Towson, MD, has evolved into a cornerstone of both Columbia and Crofton’s dining scenes. Renowned for its exceptional craft beer selection, Frisco offers a unique array of both rare and house-brewed beers, courtesy of Reckless Shepherd Brewery nestled within the same walls as Frisco Columbia.

Frisco’s inception was fueled by the founder’s culinary adventures on the West Coast after college. Inspired by the cuisine and flavors he encountered on his journey, he named the establishment after his faithful companion, Frisco, a German Shepherd whose spirit continues to infuses every corner of our establishments today.

At Frisco, we’re not just about great food and drinks; we’re about fostering connections and creating memories. Whether you’re stopping by for a cold brew after work or gathering with friends for a special occasion, we welcome you to experience the warmth and hospitality that has defined us for over two decades.
