What is iCalendar?

iCalendar allows users to import internet calendars and/or events into their personal calendar applications. You can add a single event to your calendar by selecting the iCal icon, downloading the file and importing it into your calendar program of choice. You can also subscribe to the entire calendar if you want your calendar to automatically update the calendar as events are added on the website.

Sample programs that use the iCal format include:
  • Microsoft Outlook
  • Apple iCal
  • Google Calendar
  • Most Smartphone Desktop Applications
