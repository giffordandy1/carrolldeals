Are the Throwback menu items permanent?

The Throwback Menu is a limited-time celebration of some legendary B-Dubs favorites.

How long will I be able to order the Throwback items?

Like any great comeback tour, it won't last forever. Throwback Menu items are available for a limited time, so get your favorites while they're back.

What is a Buffalo Chip?

A true B-Dubs legend. Buffalo Chips are thin-sliced potato rounds and fried until crispy. They’ve been a fan favorite for years, and now they're back where they belong.

What is Beef on Weck?

A Western New York classic. Our Beef on Weck Sliders feature tender roasted beef served on salted kummelweck-style buns.

Can I add Hot BBQ to any sauced product?

Absolutely. If Hot BBQ is your go-to, you can add it to your favorite wings and other eligible menu items wherever sauces can be selected.

Will all Buffalo Wild Wings offer the Throwback items?

Most participating Buffalo Wild Wings locations will offer the Throwback Menu. Availability may vary by location, so check your locals ports bar before ordering.

Are they available online and dine in?

Yep. Whether you're grabbing a table, ordering ahead, or browsing our App, you can enjoy Throwback Menu items however you order B-Dubs.

Can I get the Throwback products delivered?

You sure can. Throwback Menu items are available for delivery where delivery is offered. Just place your order through the Buffalo Wild Wings app or on our site.
