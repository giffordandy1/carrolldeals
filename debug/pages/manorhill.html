Family Owned, Farm Brewed

Located on a picturesque 54-acre working farm in Ellicott City, Manor Hill Farm is home to Manor Hill Brewing… Howard County’s first, and Maryland’s largest, farm brewery. Our brewing team uses high quality ingredients to craft exciting and approachable beers across both new and traditional styles.

We are open to visitors Fridays (4-9pm), Saturdays (12-7pm), and Sundays (12-7pm)…stop by the farm and have a beer!
