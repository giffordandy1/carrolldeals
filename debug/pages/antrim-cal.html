Thursday December 31st, 2026 New Year’s Eve Gala

For over 30 years, Antrim 1844 has celebrated the New Year in style, and this year, we invite you to join our cherished tradition. Say farewell to 2026 and welcome 2027 with an unforgettable night at our stunning historic mansion. Enjoy a sumptuous 7-course meal, festive décor, dancing, and live entertainment. As midnight approaches, count down to the New Year with a captivating performance by a third-generation Scottish Bagpiper. After the celebration, unwind in one of our luxurious guest rooms, complete with a cozy fireplace.

 The New Year’s Eve Package Includes:

  • Welcome Sherry
  • Lavish overnight accommodations
  • A special Antrim keepsake
  • A chilled split of Champagne awaiting you in your guest room
  • Afternoon tea with live holiday piano music
  • Passed hors d’oeuvres and a fresh seafood bar
  • A decadent six-course dining experience
  • Dancing on the veranda
  • A midnight Champagne toast led by a third-generation Scottish bagpiper
  • A hearty breakfast in the mansion with complimentary Bloody Marys or Mimosas
  • Guaranteed late checkout at noon
