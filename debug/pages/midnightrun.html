    MIDNIGHT RUN, est. 2017, came into being in a garage a long time ago by home-brewing alchemists with dreams of a brewing empire. With degrees in culinary arts & mechanical engineering, long-time friends Rich & Brent come together to create a line of dynamic & full-flavored brews.

    A passion for fine beverages; a disregard for what is; and
    a devotion for what will be.

    Our Brews

    Influenced by science & art, MIDNIGHT RUN founders create unique brews for both the experienced & inexperienced beer drinker. We pride ourselves in providing a diverse tap list that changes weekly as new beers are added. While we certainly have core beers, our interests in brewing are constantly pushing us to develop new beers and flavor experiences. When you visit our tasting room you can expect to find some combination of, but not limited to, deep, roasty stouts, fruity, medium-bodied sours, elegant golden ales, juicy IPAs, light and dark Belgian ales, gluten-reduced* seltzers, and non-alcoholic hop waters.

    MIDNIGHT RIDERS

    MIDNIGHT RIDERS don't conform to the status quo. They reject the same ol' & keep their minds & palates open.

    ​​

    As a MIDNIGHT RIDER you receive two crowler fills per month, access to limited releases, a glass of every new release on the release weekend, a birthday beer, discounts off of full pours & additional crowler fills, and more. We rely on feedback & participation from the MIDNIGHT RIDERS to drive our innovation & craft.

    ​​

    For details on pricing & the perks of becoming a MIDNIGHT RIDER inquire with one of our servers or email us at maryanne@midnightrunbrewing.com.
