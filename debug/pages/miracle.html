Miracle on Main Street

Saturday, November 28, 2026 

Whoville Event: 10:00 AM - 12:00 PM

Holiday Electric Parade: 5:00 PM - 8:00 PM


Step into a winter wonderland filled with twinkling lights, holiday music, and merry moments for the whole family! Whether you're meeting Santa, crafting holiday keepsakes, or watching the parade magic unfold, this celebration is sure to fill your season with joy!


Schedule of Events:

  • NEW CHILDREN ACTIVITY:  Festivities begin at the Westminster Family Fitness Center from 10AM - 12PM for A Little Whoville Magic.  Santa's Treat is a free children's event filled with crafts, and face painting and special guest The Grinch and Cindy Lou Who.
    • Location: Westminster Family Fitness Center 11 Longwell Ave, Westminster, MD 21157
  • 5:00 PM: Holiday Electric Parade
    • Location: Main Street, Westminster
  • Immediately following the Holiday Electric Parade: Tree Lighting Ceremony
    • Location: Locust Lane, across from 50 East Main Street, Westminster
  • Immediately following Tree Lighting Ceremony: Visits with Santa and Mrs. Claus
    • Location: Santa House, 50 East Main Street, Westminster
