Frederick's Original BrewpubCraft in Every Pint and on Every Plate

Always Iconic. Always Brewer’s.Craft Beer. Scratch Kitchen. Community Energy.

Since 1996, Brewer’s Alley has anchored Market Street as Frederick’s first brewpub, housed within a impressive landmark that has shaped the area’s history. We have spent decades perfecting the balance of revered tradition and modern vibrancy. Whether you’re here for a business lunch, a family dinner, or cocktails under the stars, our walls hold the stories of this city. Come see why the original is still the definitive Frederick experience.
