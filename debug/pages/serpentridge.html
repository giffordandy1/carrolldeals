Welcome

Serpent Ridge is a boutique vineyard and winery located in Westminster, Md. We lovingly specialize in small lots of handmade wine from grapes grown in our vineyard, select Maryland vineyards, and other carefully chosen sites. The grounds and the vineyard views provide a peaceful experience for our guests and are evidence of our attention to detail. Come visit and enjoy:

  • Award-Winning Wines
  • Beautiful Tasting Room
  • Picturesque Views
