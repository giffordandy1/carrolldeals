Year-Round & Seasonal Styles

To see a full list of our current selection including year-round, seasonal, weekly cask selection, special brews & barrel series, CLICK HERE.

Stairway IPA

7.2% ABV, 60 IBUs
India Pale Ale with Maryland Cascade hops and Chico yeast. This IPA features an earthy nose and huge malt backbone that is balanced with the citrus and spice of the hop bitterness that lingers to a dry finish.

Available year-round.

Goldie's Best Bitter

4.2% ABV, 40 IBUs
English Style Bitter with Maryland Cascade hops, Carroll County wild flower honey and Chico yeast. Traditional English Style bitter with a delicate hop balance of earth and spice.

Available year-round.

Dollyhyde Farmhouse Ale

4.5% ABV, 20 IBUs
Belgian Farmhouse with Maryland Brewers Gold hops, Carroll County wild flower honey, chamomile and Trappist Ale yeast. Light and refreshing with notes of spice and a touch of fruit

Available year-round.

Jimmy Nelly's Irish Red

5.5% ABV, 25 IBUs
This Irish Red pays homage to one of our favorite Irish farmers, Tom’s cousin Jimmy Nelly. With MD Grown two row and roasted barley From Dark Cloud, this delightfully malty Red is a true MHB favorite!

Available year-round.

Coppermine Creek Dry Stout

4.5% ABV, 40 IBUs
Dry Irish Stout with Maryland Cascade hops, roasted barley, flaked barley and Chico yeast. This traditional Dry Stout has notes of coffee and dark chocolate with low hop bitterness and a smooth and dry finish.

Available year-round.

Homestate Hefeweizen

5.3% ABV, 15 IBUs
German Style Hefeweizen with 100% Maryland ingredients with Thoroughbred 6-row pale malt and malted soft red wheat from our two local malt houses along with local Cascade and Brewers Gold hops. Our traditional Hefeweizen has a faint banana nose with soft clove on the palate and a malty wheat finish and a late honey sweetness.

Spring/Summer seasonal.

Green Farmer Pale Ale

ABV Varies, IBUs Varies

The Green Farmer Pale Ale is a rotating series featuring 100% Maryland grown ingredients. This series is brewed with seasonally available grains and hops from our local producers to truly showcase Maryland agriculture.

Available year-round.

Red Eye Porter

5.5% ABV, 40 IBUs

American Robust Porter with Maryland Cascade hops, Zeus hops, black patent malt, chocolate malt and Chico yeast. The dark chocolate and molasses nose is complimented by a slight hop fruitiness creating a comforting and complex finish with a touch of sweetness.

Fall/Winter seasonal.
