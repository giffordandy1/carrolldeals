Oyster Stroll

Saturday, October 10, 2026

12:00 - 4:00 PM

Shop, slurp, and stroll through Downtown Westminster! This annual festival features oysters, music, food and fun for the community and is designed to raise awareness about Chesapeake Bay farm-raised oysters. The event highlights how Westminster is working to restore wild oysters back into the Bay by working with environmental partners to create new oyster reefs from recycled oyster shells and concrete reef balls. 

The event directly supports CCA Maryland's Living Reef Action Campaign.


VIP Tickets: Tickets on Sale Now! You can purchase a VIP ticket by following this link!

Tickets are $40 each (or $38 each when purchasing 6 or more)
***Each table accommodates 6 seats. By purchasing 6 tickets, you are reserving an entire table. If you do not reserve the entire table, you may be seated with other VIP guests.

This package includes: 

  • 10:30 AM - 12:00 PM early access, 90-minute VIP sampling session with wineries, breweries, and distilleries
  • 12:00 – 4:00 PM exclusive, savory treat and drink 
  • Commemorative gift
  • Reserved seating for the entire day
  • Plus all general admission perks!

There are no printed tickets for this event. You'll get an email one week before the Stroll explaining the check-in process.


Gehr Lot Reserved Seating: Tickets on Sale Now! You can purchase a Gehr Lot Reserved Seating Ticket by following this link!

Tickets are $30 each (or $28 each when purchasing 6 or more)
***Each table accommodates 6 seats. By purchasing 6 tickets, you are reserving an entire table. If you do not reserve the entire table, you may be seated with other VIP guests.

This package includes: 

  • 12:00 – 4:00 PM exclusive, savory treat and drink

  • Plus all general admission perks!

General Admission: Available for purchase on event day ONLY.

Tickets are $15 each and can only be purchased at an event gate on the day of the Stroll. Guests 12 & under are free.

This package includes: 

  • 12:00 – 4:00 PM access to the stroll 
  • Live entertainment across 5 stages
  • Large yard games
  • Two tokens for two alcoholic or non-alcoholic beverages at any green Domestic Beverage Tent ONLY **Tokens are not redeemable at any other winery/brewery/distillery.**

FAQ

Where can I purchase a General Admission Ticket?

General Admission tickets will be available for purchase on event day only. Two tokens will be included with each ticket for the redemption of alcoholic or non-alcoholic beverages at a Domestic Beverage Tent ONLY. Tokens are not redeemable at any other winery/brewery/distillery.

Festival Gates Locations:

      John/Bond Street and Main Street
      Locust Lane
      Longwell Avenue and Main Street
      Center Street and Main Street

When will I get my VIP Ticket(s)?

There will be no printed tickets for this event. You will receive instruction via email one week prior to the Wine Stroll explaining the check-in process.

Where do I check-in for my VIP Ticket(s)?

VIP TICKET HOLDERS ONLY: Gate will open at 10:30 AM. Gate is located at Locust Lane behind Life Is... Pizza.

Where can I park?
