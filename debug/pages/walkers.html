Made from scratch

Enjoy incredible scratch-made American food at Walker’s Tap & Table, as well as live music, cocktails and an exhaustive list of draft beers. Executive Chef Chad Wells’ offbeat approach to traditional fare combines familiar favorites with new and exciting flavors. Our menu frequently changes, so be sure to check back often to see what’s on our plates!
