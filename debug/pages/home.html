Only The Freshest & Most Flavorful

Whether you choose to take full advantage of the full Basta Pasta experience at our flagship restaurant in Eldersburg there is one guarantee that you & your family will always have: only the freshest & most flavorful selection of authentic Italian cuisine & seafood, always prepared with the finest ingredients for a masterful presentation.

Menu

Our wide selection of Italian entrees offer only the freshest and most flavorful ingredients, from our signature pastas to a selection of various seafood dishes that incorporate Maryland’s best catches – including our renowned crab cakes!

Catering

Since our founding, we have proudly offered services for all of your take-out catering needs, and the Eldersburg catering menu includes all of the signature Italian cuisine that will truly take our taste of Italy from Basta Pasta to your own family event.

Gift Cards

We want to provide as many options to share our dining experience for you and your family, and offer Gift Cards which make the perfect gift to celebrate anniversaries, birthdays, or as a truly memorable night with someone special.
