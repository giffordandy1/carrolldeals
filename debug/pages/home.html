Tap Into Something Great.

Barrington Tap & Table brings together craft beer, scratch-made American cuisine, live music, and expertly crafted cocktails in a space that’s both welcoming and refined. Our Chef takes a creative approach to classic favorites, blending familiar flavors with fresh, unexpected twists. With a menu that evolves regularly, there’s always something new to discover so be sure to check back often to see what we’re serving next.

Comfort Food, Perfectly Crafted

Barrington Tap & Table offers a refined yet relaxed dining experience where elevated comfort meets thoughtful craftsmanship. Our menu features a curated selection of small plates, fresh salads, brick oven pizzas, and satisfying mains which are perfect for sharing or enjoying solo.
