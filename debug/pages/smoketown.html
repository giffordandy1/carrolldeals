    Smoketown Brewing is Brunswick, Maryland’s hometown spot for fresh beer, good food, and easygoing hangs—built inside a restored downtown fire station with plenty of character.

     

    We brew a rotating lineup of craft beers, pour a full bar, and keep the calendar lively with live music, entertainment, and community events throughout the year.

    ​

    The “Smoketown” name is a nod to Brunswick’s railroad roots and the trains that still roll through town today—part of the backdrop you’ll catch from the patio on any given day.

    ​

    Settle into the taproom for a pint, grab a table for dinner, or head out to our spacious dog-friendly patio with views of the bridge and passing trains. Whether you're meeting friends for a casual night out or spending the afternoon with family, there’s always a place to relax and enjoy the atmosphere.

    ​

    Looking for a place to celebrate? Smoketown Brewing is also home to Upstairs at Smoketown, our beautifully renovated second-floor event hall. Featuring historic charm, modern amenities, and space for up to 400, Upstairs at Smoketown is a performance oriented venue for ticketed shows, but is also available to host private parties, corporate events, fundraisers, and community gatherings.

     

    The venue offers a unique setting in the heart of downtown Brunswick, with original architectural details that reflect the building’s history while providing a warm and inviting space for memorable occasions.

    ​

    Come by year-round for something new on tap, a seat with a view, and the kind of place that feels like Brunswick. Whether you're joining us for a beer, a meal, a concert, or a special event upstairs, you'll find a welcoming place built around good company and local character.
