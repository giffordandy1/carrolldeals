Yoga & Mimosas: A Brunch Experience

Grab your friends and join us for the ultimate Sunday morning reset! ☀️🥂

Start your day with a yoga class led by Table2Mat Yoga, followed by a delicious brunch experience!

📅 Event Details

  • 📆 Date: Sunday, November 15, 2026
  • 🧘 Yoga: The Yard
  • 🍽️ Brunch: The Yard
  • ⏰ Yoga Class: 10:00 AM – 11:00 AM (Check-in starts at 9:30 AM)
  • 🥂 Brunch: Immediately following class

🎟️ Your $50 Ticket Includes

  • 🧘‍♀️ One-hour beginner-friendly yoga class
  • 🥂 Two mimosas
  • 🍳 Brunch entrée — choose your favorite brunch item from the menu below after class.

Naughty & Dice: A Holiday Casino Night

This holiday season, we’re trading the usual holiday party for something a little more Naughty.

Join us December 5, 2026 for Naughty & Dice: A Holiday Casino Night—an elevated evening of incredible food, cocktails, casino games, music, and a little friendly competition, all while giving back to those in need in our community.

Start the night with champagne, cocktails, and gourmet hors d’oeuvres in our holiday pop-up lounge before heading out to The Yard for casino games. Try your luck at the tables, enjoy beer, wine, and cocktails throughout the evening, then finish the night by hitting the dance floor with our DJ.

General Admission

$150 per person

General Admission includes all hors d’oeuvres, beer, wine, cocktails, and a set amount of casino chips to use on the casino floor for the evening.

VIP Experience
Want to take your night up a level? Reserve a VIP table for your group and enjoy a few extra perks.

4-Person VIP Table | $850
6-Person VIP Table | $1,275
8-Person VIP Table | $1,700
12-Person VIP Table + Private Craps Table | $3,500
a set amount of casino chips

VIP tables include admission for your full table, all hors d’oeuvres, beer, wine and cocktails, casino chips, access to private VIP games, welcome champagne at your table, and dedicated VIP table service.

Dress up. Drink up. Place your bets.

A portion of the evening will benefit those in need within our community, making this one holiday party that feels just as good as it looks.

All ticket sales are final and non-refundable.
