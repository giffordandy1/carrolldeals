Outside Food Policy

No outside food is permitted in our taproom or on our patio when a food truck is on site. If you are hungry, we encourage you to order from our food truck partner.

Feel free to check ahead! Call us to confirm our daily food truck schedule.

For last-minute updates/cancellations, check our Instagram and Facebook stories!
