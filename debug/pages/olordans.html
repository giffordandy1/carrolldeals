The Irish Experience

A small slice of Ireland in downtown Westminster, MD. Raise a pint in the pub, enjoy the fireplace in the pub area, dine in one of our two dining rooms or dine Alfresco on one of our two outdoor decks.  We have both Irish fare as well as classic American cuisine. We have a full service bar and inside and during the summer months we also have a full service bar outside. We can accommodate large parties and small groups.  We are family oriented and enjoy having small children in the restaurant and pups on the patio. We also can offer private functions with on-site catering for rehearsal dinners, bridal showers, baby showers, wakes, birthday parties and so much more. We strongly recommend reservations for all size groups.
Please contact us at  410-876-0000.
